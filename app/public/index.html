Questions worth answering

Where do the salary numbers come from?

Live postings that state a salary, refreshed continuously, annualised to EUR. Nothing is self-reported and nothing is a survey. Every figure shows its sample size and confidence, and a segment with too few comparables is hidden rather than guessed.

Is this a job board?

No. A job board answers what exists. ThinkZet answers what you are worth, what you are missing, and which move pays. Listings are an input, not the product.

Do you sell my data or show it to recruiters?

No. Not to employers, not to recruiters, not to anyone. Your CV and salary are never shared. You can export or delete everything at any time.

Do referral partners change what I see?

Never. A commission cannot alter your score, your ranking or which opportunities appear. That constraint is enforced in code, not just in policy, and every partner link is labelled.

What if the number is wrong for me?

Tell us. Every benchmark carries its evidence, and a wrong number is more useful to us than a flattering one. Corrections are how the model sharpens.
