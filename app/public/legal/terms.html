Terms

Plain language, because terms nobody reads protect nobody.

What we provide

Karyvo is an information and intelligence service. We aggregate opportunity data from public sources, estimate what your profile is worth in the market, and score opportunities against you. We are not a recruitment agency, an employer, a job board, or a career adviser acting on your behalf.

What we do not promise

  • We do not promise you a job. We estimate probabilities, and estimates are sometimes wrong.
  • We do not promise the numbers are exact. Benchmarks are computed from postings that disclose pay, which is a sample, not a census. Every figure shows its confidence and sample size.
  • We do not promise listings are live. Sources change and roles close. We filter stale and ghost listings continuously, but cannot guarantee perfection.

Your account

You are responsible for the accuracy of what you tell us — the scores are only as good as the profile behind them. Keep your email secure; anyone with access to your inbox can sign in.

Subscriptions

  • You can cancel at any time; access continues until the end of the paid period.
  • If a payment fails, access is suspended until it succeeds.
  • Prices may change with 30 days' notice; existing subscribers keep their price for the current period.

Referral partners

Some recommendations route to partners who may pay us a commission. Every one is labelled. A commission never changes what we recommend, how it is scored, or how it is ranked. If a partner's offer is not the best fit for you, we will say so.

Acceptable use

Do not scrape us at scale, resell access, attempt to re-identify other users from aggregated data, or use the service to build a competing dataset. We may suspend accounts that do.

Liability
