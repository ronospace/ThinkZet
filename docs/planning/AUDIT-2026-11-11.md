# ThinkZet – full product audit

Every conclusion below was measured in a headless browser at 1440×900 against the running build, or read from the endpoint the
screen calls. Nothing here is inferred from a screenshot.

Audit date: 11 November 2026 · `master` at `0ef48e1` · tagged `restore-20261111-39`

## How each screen was judged

1. Who is this for?
2. What question should it answer?
3. Is the answer visible within a few seconds?
4. Is the information ordered correctly?
5. Is anything unnecessary, duplicated or confusing?
6. Does the hierarchy match the importance?

## Audience map, as it stands

| Group | Screens | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| User | Daily Brief, Opportunity Radar, Career Score, Rate Intelligence, Market Pulse, Negotiation, Trajectory, Skill Gaps, Career Tracker, Copilot, Saved Searches, Career Twin, Learning Loop, Partners, Plan | Correctly grouped in Today, Intelligence, Act, Account |
| Founder | Funnel, Leads, Email, Launch, Engine | Now in their own Operations group, hidden from everyone else |
| Business | Partnerships, CPA, revshare, routing, payouts | **Does not exist as a screen.** Partners shows referral programmes, not commercial terms |
