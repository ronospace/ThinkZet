# Karyvo — Mission Plan & Operating Priorities

**Last updated:** 7 November 2026
**Single question every piece of work must answer:** *Will this help us reach the first 100 paying users faster?*

---

## Mission

> Make every person's next career decision measurably better — with intelligence, not listings.

**Positioning:** Opportunity Intelligence Platform and Career Operating System.
Not a job board. Not a CV builder. Not LinkedIn. Not Mercor.

**Primary value proposition:** *Discover how much opportunity you're leaving on the table.*
**Secondary:** *One profile. Every opportunity.*

---

## The five things we optimise for (in order)

1. **Activation** — a new visitor reaches their first personal insight fast
2. **Retention** — they come back without being pushed
3. **Value creation** — they measurably earn more, or waste less effort
4. **Revenue diversification** — never one revenue stream
5. **Product-market fit** — proven by retention and payment, not by opinions

**Explicitly not optimised for:** feature count.

---

## Revenue model — priority order

| # | Stream | Status | Why this order |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Referral revenue** | Scaffolded | Highest margin (95%), aligned with advice, works at any scale, needs no inventory |
| 2 | **Subscription** | Live (Stripe test) | Predictable, but alone it caps the business and fights honesty |
| 3 | **Marketplace** (coaches, CV reviewers, immigration, relocation) | Designed | Rides on intent the product already creates |
| 4 | **Recruiter revenue** | Not started | Requires user trust first; conflicts with neutrality if early |
| 5 | **Employer revenue** | Not started | Last. Destroying neutrality too early would kill the moat |

**Referral principle applied throughout:** when the product identifies a gap (e.g. "you're missing AWS certification"), the fix should route to a partner *and* be disclosed as one — never silently, and never ranked higher because of it.

---

## Product priorities — double down on the four that work

1. **Rate Intelligence** — the emotional hook and the most defensible data
2. **Career Score** — the strategic growth asset
3. **Opportunity Radar** — the daily habit
4. **Career Twin** — the switching cost

Everything else supports these four.

## Standing rules

- **Preserve simplicity.** Every new surface must earn its place.
- **Activation first.** A feature nobody reaches is a feature that does not exist.
- **Retention over acquisition.** Fix the leak before filling the bucket.
- **Evidence over instruction.** If the data says a different path is better, say so, explain why, and build that instead.
- **One revenue stream is a single point of failure.**
- **Never let referral money touch ranking.** Enforced in code, not policy.
