not estimated.

## 3. Nothing moves itself

`screenFlip` is deleted, not disabled: the wheel listener is gone from the source, so no event can
reach it. `html.is-landing body` is `scroll-snap-type:none` with `scroll-behavior:auto`.

Proof: at 1440, the Radar was scrolled to its end and given two further wheel gestures of 120px.
`STATE.view` was `radar` before and `radar` after.
