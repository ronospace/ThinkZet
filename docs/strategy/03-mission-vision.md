# Mission & Vision

## Mission (what we do every day)

> **Make every person's next career decision measurably better — with intelligence, not listings.**

The test we apply to every feature: *does this help someone make a better decision about their career, income, or life in the next 30 days?* If not, we don't build it.

## Vision (what we become)

> **The Bloomberg Terminal for careers — the intelligence layer between talent and every opportunity in the world.**

A Bloomberg terminal is not valuable because it lists prices. It's valuable because it:
- **normalises** a chaotic universe of instruments,
- **prices** them,
- **scores** them against your portfolio and constraints,
- and gives you a **decision surface** with an opinion.

ThinkZet becomes that for the 4 billion people whose most valuable asset is their labour and their next move:

| Bloomberg does for | ThinkZet does for |
|---|---|
| securities | opportunities |
| market data | market + salary + skills data |
| portfolio fit | Career Twin fit |
| price signals | Opportunity Score / Match Score |
| analyst notes | AI Career Copilot |
| terminal | Opportunity Radar |

## The three-horizon vision

| Horizon | Years | What we are | Who pays |
|---|---|---|---|
| **H1: The Decision Layer** | 0–2 | The best personal opportunity intelligence for individual professionals | Individuals (subscription) + referral partners |
| **H2: The Talent Graph** | 2–4 | The proprietary graph of who can do what, who wants what, and what outcomes follow — licensed and queried by employers, educators, immigration and relocation providers | Individuals + institutions (data/API/SaaS) |
| **H3: The Opportunity Rail** | 4–7 | The settlement layer where opportunities are matched, funded, and fulfilled — talent-side economics on a global scale | Marketplaces, employers, governments |

## What we are explicitly *not*

This section exists because every failed "super app for careers" died from scope.

- **Not a job board.** We don't optimise for listing count. We optimise for decisions made and outcomes achieved.
- **Not a recruitment agency.** We never place a candidate for a fee in our first two years. We route; we don't recruit.
- **Not a CV builder.** CV is an input, not the product.
- **Not a social network.** No feed of other people's thoughts. No DMs. No community in the MVP.
- **Not a marketplace in year one.** Marketplaces need liquidity; we need to earn the right to have liquidity first.

## Values (the operating system of the company)

1. **Decisions over data.** A score with a reason beats a list of 10,000 rows.
2. **Outcomes over engagement.** We optimise for "user got the thing," not "user opened the app 14 times." The north star is **Verified Outcomes**, not DAU.
3. **Honest scores.** A match score that flatters is worthless. We will show people "you will probably not get this," and that honesty *is* the product.
4. **Solo-scale simplicity.** Every feature must survive being maintained by one person for 18 months.
5. **Data dignity.** The user's career data is theirs. Export, delete, and port at any time. Trust is the only real moat in a category full of spam.

## North star metric

> **Verified Positive Outcomes per 100 active users per month** (an interview, an offer, a signed contract, a fellowship, a visa approval, a rate increase).

Everything else — DAU, MRR, referral clicks — is a leading indicator of this.
