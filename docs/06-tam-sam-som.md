# TAM / SAM / SOM

All figures in EUR. Bottom-up where possible, top-down cross-checked. Assumptions are stated explicitly so they can be attacked.

---

## 1. TAM — Total Addressable Market

### Definition
Global spend on **career decision-making** by individuals and the institutions that serve them: subscriptions for career intelligence, career services/coaching, learning tied to career moves, and the referral/intermediation value flowing from career decisions.

### Top-down

| Layer | Global value | Source logic |
|---|---|---|
| Global job-board + careers advertising | ~$28B | Ad spend + listings revenue |
| Global career coaching / services / outplacement | ~$12B | Fragmented, offline-heavy |
| Global professional learning & certification | ~$46B (professional segment ~$18B) | Coursera/Udemy/cert vendors |
| AI talent marketplace take-rate | ~$6–9B | 15–30% of GMV |
| **Addressable "decision-layer" slice** | **$8–12B** | 8–12% of the above is spend attributable to *deciding* rather than *transacting* |

**TAM (2026): ~€9–11B.** Growing at 14–18% CAGR, with the decision slice growing faster than the underlying markets because AI is increasing decision frequency (more options per person).

### Bottom-up cross-check (individual subscriptions)

## 2. SAM — Serviceable Addressable Market

### Definition
The portion ThinkZet can realistically serve **within 3 years** with its product surface, languages, geographies, and go-to-market: English-speaking + EU professionals in AI/tech-adjacent knowledge work, reachable digitally, with a cross-border or multi-opportunity career reality.

| Filter | Value |
|---|---|
| Global knowledge workers in tech/AI-adjacent roles | ~48M |
| × Digitally reachable (English or EU language, active on modern platforms) | 65% → 31M |
| × Has a genuine *multi-opportunity* decision problem (contractor, relocating, pivoting) | 30% → **9.3M people** |
| × Realistic 3-year penetration (paid + free monetisable) | 1.5% → **140k paying users** |
| × Blended ARPU (subscription + referral + services share) | €290/yr |
| **SAM (3-year revenue potential)** | **≈ €41M ARR** |

## 3. SOM — Serviceable Obtainable Market

### Definition
What a **solo-founder-led, €0-budget, 3-year** company can realistically capture. Constrained by distribution, not by market.

| Year | Registered users | Paying subs (exit) | ARR (exit) | Market share of SAM |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Y1 | 18,000 | 700 | €172k | 0.4% |
| Y2 | 140,000 | 6,200 | €1.45M | 3.5% |
| Y3 | 620,000 | 31,000 | €7.4M | 18% |

**SOM (Year 3) ≈ €7.4M ARR**, ~18% of SAM. Deliberately conservative on share, aggressive on growth rate (a validated category leader in a niche can hold 20–40% of its niche).

### SOM sensitivity

| Scenario | Y3 ARR | Key assumption |
|---|---|---|
| **Bear** | €2.8M | D30 retention 28%, CAC €55, no referral revenue |
| **Base** | €7.4M | D30 retention 45%, CAC €28, referral = 22% of revenue |
| **Bull** | €16.5M | D30 retention 55%, institutional deals land, 2 small-team hires |

### The "venture-scale" test

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Can this reach €10M ARR? | Yes — base case Y3 is €7.4M, bull is €16.5M. |
| Can this reach €100M ARR? | Only via H2 (Talent Graph / institutional data licensing) and H3 (opportunity rail). The individual subscription business alone tops out at ~€40–60M ARR. |
| Is the TAM big enough for a fund-returning outcome? | Yes if the decision layer becomes infrastructure (H2). Marginal if it stays a consumer subscription. **This must be explicit in any raise.** |

## 4. The honest market-sizing caveat

Most pitch decks in this space inflate TAM by using "global recruitment market $200B+." That number is employer spend on hiring — **not** individual spend on career decisions. Using it is a credibility killer with good investors.

Our defensible claim: **€9–11B TAM, €41M SAM, €7.4M SOM by Year 3**, with a credible path to €30–100M ARR via institutional expansion. That is a real venture-scale company if — and only if — we own the decision layer.
