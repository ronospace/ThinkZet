# Investor Pitch Narrative

> Written as a 12-slide narrative + the spoken script. Optimised for a pre-seed / seed conversation with an operator-investor who has seen 200 "AI career" decks and is allergic to them.

---

## Slide 1 — Title

**MirrorSpread AI**
*The Operating System for Career Growth*
One Profile. Every Opportunity.

---

## Slide 2 — The line that opens the meeting

> **"The internet solved finding opportunities. It never solved choosing between them. There are 89 billion dollars of transactions sitting behind a decision nobody has built software for."**

**Script:**
> "Every professional today has more opportunities than ever — a job, two contracts, a fellowship, a visa route, a co-founder conversation — and zero tooling to compare them. LinkedIn shows you 10,000 jobs. It never tells you which one you should take. That's not a search problem. It's a decision problem. And it's completely unowned."

---

## Slide 3 — The problem, quantified

| The user's week | Time | Money |
|---|---|---|
| Manual search across 15 platforms | 6–8 hrs | — |
| Duplicate applications, same CV, same forms | 3 hrs | — |
| Rate blindness (typically 20–40% underpaid on ≥1 channel) | — | €8–25k/yr |
| Missed opportunities (close in <72 hrs) | — | €5–20k/yr |
| Wrong-direction moves (bad-fit role, dead-end contract) | — | 1–3 years of career compounding |

**Script:**
> "The average professional spends a full workday every week on a decision that determines 60% of their lifetime income — and does it with worse tools than they'd use to buy a flight."

---

## Slide 4 — The insight

> **Job boards are single-inventory and ad-funded. That makes them structurally unable to be honest. A decision layer must be neutral, personal, and paid by the user.**

Three consequences:
1. No incumbent can build it (they'd cannibalise their own inventory).
2. It requires *personal* context — goals, constraints, visa status, risk tolerance.
3. It requires *outcome data* — which only compounds if you ask "did you get it?"

**Script:**
> "This isn't a feature LinkedIn forgot. It's a feature LinkedIn can't build. If LinkedIn told you 'don't apply to this job, apply to that fellowship instead,' it would be telling you to leave LinkedIn. The conflict is structural. That's the opening."

---

## Slide 5 — The product

**MirrorSpread = one profile → a ranked, explained, tracked opportunity pipeline across 15 opportunity types.**

| Layer | Product | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Ingest | Opportunity Radar™ | Scans 15+ sources continuously; entity-resolves and deduplicates |
| Understand | Career Twin™ | Persistent model of the person: skills, goals, constraints, history |
| Score | Opportunity Match Engine™ | Match, income potential, interview probability, offer probability, missing skills |
| Measure | Career Score™ | 6 scores: employability, interview readiness, market demand, salary potential, growth, momentum |
| Decide | Opportunity Dashboard™ | Market, salary and skill intelligence in one screen |
| Act | AI Career Copilot™ | CV, cover letters, interview prep, negotiation, learning plans |

**The demo moment (30 seconds):**
> "Good morning, Geoffrey. 27 opportunities detected. 4 remote AI roles, 3 Flutter contracts, 2 relocation roles in Germany, 1 fellowship. Highest Opportunity Score: 96. And here's the one thing that matters: *you were offered €75/hr last month. Your market rate is €95–120/hr. Here's the three contracts that pay it.*"

**Script:**
> "That last line is the whole company. It's not information. It's a decision, with a number attached, that changes what someone does on Monday morning."

## The five questions an investor will ask (and the answers)

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| **"Isn't this a GPT wrapper?"** | "The LLM is 10% of the work. The other 90% is entity resolution across 15 sources, deduplication, calibration against real outcomes, and a neutral position that incumbents can't take. A wrapper has no data flywheel; we do." |
| **"Won't LinkedIn just do this?"** | "They can't be neutral. Their revenue depends on ranking their own inventory. It's a business-model conflict, not an engineering gap." |
| **"Career products churn when people get jobs."** | "Yes — that's why we sell annual, why Career Twin continues post-offer (next-move radar, salary tracking), and why reactivation at 6 months is our cheapest channel. Our churn assumption of 5.5%/mo is deliberately conservative." |
| **"Why won't users just use ChatGPT?"** | "They can, for one-off advice. They can't get: a continuously updated cross-platform feed, calibrated probabilities, outcome-informed benchmarks, or tracking. ChatGPT is a consultant with no data; we're a system with data." |
| **"What's the wedge?"** | "Global AI contractors. They're easy to reach, feel the rate-blindness pain acutely, have high WTP, and their outcome (a rate increase) is measurable in euros within weeks." |
