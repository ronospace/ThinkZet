# Karyvo AI — Executive Summary

**Date:** 7 November 2026
**Status:** Pre-seed / validation
**Category:** Opportunity Operating System (not a job board)

---

## The one-sentence thesis

> Job boards answer *"what exists?"*. Karyvo answers *"what should **I** do next, and why?"* — and then runs the pipeline for you.

The internet solved opportunity **discovery** in 2008 (Indeed, LinkedIn). It never solved opportunity **decisioning**. Every platform added more listings; nobody added a decision layer. That is the gap.

## What we are building

Karyvo AI is a personal **opportunity intelligence system**. A user connects one profile (CV + goals + constraints), and Karyvo:

1. **Radar** — continuously scans the market across 15+ heterogeneous opportunity types (jobs, contracts, fellowships, grants, visas, co-founder matches) and produces a ranked daily feed.
2. **Match Engine** — scores every opportunity on match, income potential, interview probability, offer probability, and realistic-ness.
3. **Career Twin** — a persistent model of the person that improves with every application, interview and outcome.
4. **Career Score** — six proprietary scores (employability, interview readiness, market demand, salary potential, growth, momentum).
5. **Dashboard** — market, salary and skill intelligence in one screen.
6. **Copilot** — CV, cover letter, interview coaching, negotiation, learning plans.

## Why now (the three shifts)

| Shift | What changed | Why it unlocks Karyvo |
|---|---|---|
| **AI-native labour market** | LLM evaluation platforms (Mercor, micro1, Turing, Surge, Scale) turned talent into a global commodity priced per hour — and *re-priced* it every 6 months. | People now have 10–50 realistic income options at any moment and zero tooling to compare them. Comparison, not discovery, is the bottleneck. |
| **AI made the data layer cheap** | Parsing a CV, normalising a listing, scoring fit and predicting salary used to cost a data-science team; now it's an LLM call + an embedding. | A solo founder can ship a credible decision engine in 30–60 days. Previously this needed $3–5M. |
| **Trust collapse in listings** | Ghost jobs, recruiter spam, and AI-generated applications have made job boards low-signal. | A curated, scored, *explained* feed is now more valuable than a bigger one. Quality is the new distribution. |

## The ask / what we need next

This is a **validation plan first, capital plan second**.

- **Next 30 days:** ship the MVP, get 100 paying users, prove the feed is better than LinkedIn's.
- **Next 90 days:** €10k MRR, referral partnerships signed, retention curve proven at D30 ≥ 45%.
- **Pre-seed (optional, month 6):** €750k–€1.5M on a 6.6x LTV/CAC and a 45%+ D30 retention story. Not needed to reach €10k MRR.

## The brutal one-liner

Karyvo is worth building **only if** we win the *decision* layer, not the *listing* layer. If we end up as "LinkedIn with better filters," we are dead. Every design decision in this document is filtered through one question: **does this make the user's next career decision measurably better?**
