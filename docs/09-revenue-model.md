# Revenue Model & Pricing

## 0. Pricing philosophy

Three rules:

1. **Charge for decisions, not access.** Access to listings is free everywhere. Decisions are worth money.
2. **The first "holy shit" insight is free.** The rate-gap or visa-qualification finding must happen before the paywall.
3. **Price against the value of one better decision, not against competitor subscriptions.** One 10% rate improvement on a €70k salary = €7,000. €149/year is 2% of that.

## 2. Stream 1 — CareerOS Pro (core)

### Tiers

| Tier | Price | What's included | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Free** | €0 | 1 CV upload, 5 match scores/mo, daily feed (top 10), basic rate check, application tracker (5 items) | Acquisition + the "holy shit" moment |
| **Pro** | **€19/mo** or **€149/yr** (35% annual discount) | Unlimited match scores, full Radar, Career Twin, all 6 Career Scores, Copilot (CV/cover letter/interview/negotiation), salary intelligence, unlimited tracking | **Core revenue** |
| **Pro+ (Year 2)** | €39/mo | Everything + 1:1 coach credits, relocation readiness report, priority opportunity alerts, multi-profile (job + freelance) | Expansion revenue |
| **Teams / Cohort (Yr 2)** | €12/seat/mo (min 10) | For bootcamps, universities, agencies | Institutional wedge |
| **Regional pricing** | 45–70% of Pro | India/SEA/LATAM/Africa (PPP-adjusted): €7–9/mo | Volume + fairness |

### Why €19 / €149

| Anchor | Value |
|---|---|
| One missed €90/hr contract (4 weeks) | €14,400 |
| One 10% rate correction on €70k | €7,000/yr |
| FlexJobs subscription | $15–25/mo (proven WTP for *curation alone*) |
| LinkedIn Premium | €30–60/mo (proven WTP for *no decision layer*) |
| A single career coach session | €150–300 |

**€19/mo is deliberately below LinkedIn Premium.** We want "obviously worth it," not "considered purchase." €149/yr converts the emotionally-convinced user into 12 months of retained revenue and cash upfront.

### Subscription economics

| Metric | Target (M6) | Target (M18) |
|---|---|---|
| Free → Pro conversion | 3.5% | 5.0% |
| Monthly churn (Pro) | 7.5% | 5.5% |
| Annual plan share | 30% | 45% |
| ARPU (blended, monthly) | €16.20 | €18.90 |
| D30 retention (free) | 35% | 45% |
| D90 retention (Pro) | 68% | 78% |

### The churn problem and its structural fix

Career products churn when the user gets a job. This is the single biggest business-model risk. Three fixes:

1. **Annual-first framing** ("the whole search, one price") — most users get a job in 3–6 months, so annual captures the full value period.
2. **Post-win value:** Career Twin keeps working after the offer — salary tracking, next-move radar, skill maintenance. The product's job is to make you *never* want to cancel.
3. **Reactivation loop:** when a user cancels with "I got a job," we ask for the outcome (moat data), then set a 6-month "next move" reminder. Reactivation is our cheapest acquisition channel.

## 3. Stream 2 — Referral & affiliate revenue

### The model

When a user's best-matched opportunity is on a partner platform with a referral programme, we route them with tracking and earn a bounty or revenue share. **This is only ever shown when it's genuinely the best option** — otherwise it destroys the neutrality moat, which is worth more than the referral fee.

### Partner categories and realistic economics

| Category | Example partners | Payout (typical) | Qualified action |
|---|---|---|---|
| AI-talent marketplaces | Mercor, micro1, Turing, Arc | €50–€400 per hire/engagement | Vetted + placed |
| Job boards / niche networks | Otta, Wellfound, niche boards | €10–€80 per qualified signup | Activated candidate |
| Training & certification | Coursera, DataCamp, Udemy, AWS, Microsoft, Google Cloud | 10–30% of course price (€10–€150) | Purchase |
| Coaching | Independent coaches, platforms | 20–30% of session | Booked session |
| Immigration / relocation | Visa consultancies, relocation services | €50–€500 per consultation | Qualified lead |
| Recruitment agencies (specialist) | Niche recruiters | €200–€800 per placement | Placed candidate |

### Referral revenue model (conservative)

| Assumption | Value |
|---|---|
| Monthly active users (Y2 avg) | 45,000 |
| % who take a referred action / month | 4.5% |
| Referred actions / month | 2,025 |
| Blended payout per action | €28 |
| **Referral revenue / month (Y2)** | **€56,700** |
| Referral revenue / month (Y3, 130k MAU) | ~€164,000 |

**Realistic Y1 referral revenue: €28k. Y2: €520k. Y3: €1.4M.**

### Referral integrity rules (non-negotiable)

1. Referral links never affect ranking or scores.
2. Every referral is labelled and disclosed.
3. If a non-partner opportunity scores higher, the non-partner opportunity wins.
4. We publish this policy. It is a marketing asset.

## 4. Stream 3 — Career Services marketplace

**Model:** Vetted providers (career coaches, CV experts, recruiters, interview trainers, immigration and relocation specialists) listed with transparent pricing. **20% take rate**, provider sets price.

| Service | Typical price | Take (20%) |
|---|---|---|
| CV/Career Twin deep review | €79 | €15.80 |
| Interview coaching (1 session) | €120 | €24 |
| Career strategy session | €150 | €30 |
| Immigration consultation | €200 | €40 |
| Relocation readiness package | €450 | €90 |

**Y2 volume assumption:** 900 bookings/yr → €220k GMV → **€44k net revenue.** Y3: 3,500 bookings → €850k GMV → **€170k net.**

**Why 20% and not 30%:** we're the demand side, not the supply side. Providers are fragmented and will accept 20% for qualified, high-intent leads. Higher take rates push them to route around us.

## 5. Stream 4 — Learning marketplace

**Model:** Rev-share with course/certification providers, recommended *only* when a skill gap is detected and the course is genuinely the shortest path.

| Assumption | Value |
|---|---|
| Users with an active skill gap (Y2) | 55% of MAU |
| Course click-through | 6% |
| Purchase rate | 4% |
| Avg course price | €180 |
| Rev share | 20% |
| **Y2 net revenue** | ~€21k |
| **Y3 net revenue** | ~€180k |

Learning revenue is small but strategically valuable: it's the *proof* that the skill-gap engine works, and it deepens the outcome loop ("did the course close the gap?").

## 7. Pricing experiments to run (in order)

1. **€19 vs €24 vs €29/mo** — find the price elasticity ceiling in weeks 1–8. (n=300)
2. **Paywall placement:** after 3 scored opportunities vs after 5 vs after the first rate-gap insight.
3. **Annual discount depth:** 35% vs 45%.
4. **"Outcome guarantee":** 90-day refund if no interview. Tests trust and forces honest scoring. Run at M4.
5. **Regional PPP pricing** — launch India/Brazil/Nigeria at €9 from month 2 (they're a large part of P1/P2).

## 8. Revenue model verdict

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Is subscription the right core? | Yes. It aligns us with the user, enables honesty, and yields 88% margins. |
| Is referral revenue a distraction? | Only if it's allowed to influence ranking. Handled with hard integrity rules. |
| Do we need the marketplace? | Not for €10k MRR. It's a month 5+ expansion to raise ARPU and retention. |
| What's the riskiest assumption? | Free→paid conversion at 3.5%+ and D30 retention at 35%+. Both must be validated in the first 8 weeks. |
| Can this reach €10k MRR without a marketplace or referral revenue? | Yes: ~530 Pro subscribers. That is the true milestone. |
